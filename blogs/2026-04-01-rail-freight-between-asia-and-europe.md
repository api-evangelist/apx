APX Distribution Reimagined Rail Freight Between Asia and Europe Global logistics conditions have shifted significantly in recent years. Rising fuel prices linked to geopolitical tensions and conflict in key energy regions have increased transportation costs across all modes (Reuters). At the same time, maritime disruptions have forced vessels to reroute, extending transit times between Asia and Europe by up to 10–20 days (SeaRates).
